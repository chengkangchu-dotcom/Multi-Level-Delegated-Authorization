# Proposed deliverable

The proposed outcome is one **Technical Report** analysing multi-level delegated authorization for AI agents. It will cover use cases, the three core requirements, a reference model and the gap analysis of existing standards and proposals.

This report is intended to contribute to Annex A.1.2 of the FG-TIDA Terms of Reference, on the properties of a delegation artefact, and to the use-case and requirements reports in clause 4.1 [P5].

| Stage | Work |
|---|---|
| 1. Scope and requirements | Consolidate delegation use cases and the three core requirements. |
| 2. Report drafting | Develop the reference model, compare existing standards and proposals, and draft the gap analysis. |
| 3. Report finalization | Incorporate FG-TIDA feedback and finalize the proposed Technical Report. |

The FG-TIDA Terms of Reference require the Focus Group to submit a final report, including its complete set of deliverables, to SG17. This work item proposes one technical contribution to that set; its acceptance and inclusion remain subject to FG-TIDA agreement [P5].
