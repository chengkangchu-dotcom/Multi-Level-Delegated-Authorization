Title: Multi-Level Delegated Authorization

## Abstract

AI agents may delegate tasks across service providers and trust domains. This contribution proposes an Incubation work item combining monotonic permission attenuation, binding to the authorized agent's identity and key, and selective disclosure of authorization information.

An authorizing principal establishes a root grant; each authorized agent may derive narrower grants for designated downstream agents. Verifiers check root authority, delegation evidence, proof of key possession and applicable constraints. A two-level user-authorized flow illustrates the model without prescribing a root-issuance mechanism. The proposed outcome is a Technical Report covering use cases, requirements, a reference model and gap analysis.
